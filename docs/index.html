01What is TRIZ?TRIZ 是什么

TRIZ — the Theory of Inventive Problem Solving — was distilled by Genrich Altshuller and his colleagues starting in 1946, from the analysis of hundreds of thousands of patents. Its core insight: innovation is not random genius. The same technical problems recur across industries, and their solutions follow reusable patterns. A problem solved in aerospace in 1975 may be the same contradiction a battery engineer faces today.

TRIZ（俄文"发明问题解决理论"缩写）由根里奇·阿奇舒勒及其同事自 1946 年起，从对数十万件专利的分析中提炼而成。它的核心洞察是：创新不是随机的天才灵光——同样的技术问题在不同行业反复出现，而它们的解法遵循可复用的模式。航空领域 1975 年解决的问题，可能就是今天电池工程师面对的同一个矛盾。

The toolbox is unusually structured for a creativity method: 40 numbered inventive principles and the contradiction matrix; separation principles for physical contradictions; ARIZ, a full problem-solving algorithm; and the laws of technical-system evolution. Samsung, Siemens, GE and Boeing have trained thousands of engineers on it. The catch: today it lives almost entirely inside expensive courses and certified consultants — a few thousand experts worldwide, against millions of engineers who could use it.

它的工具箱在"创造力方法"里罕见地结构化：40 个有编号的发明原理与矛盾矩阵、分离原理、完整的 ARIZ 算法、技术系统进化法则。三星、西门子、GE、波音都曾用它内训数以千计的工程师。问题是：这套方法论今天几乎只存在于昂贵的培训课程和持证咨询师身上——全球活跃咨询师千人量级，而需要它的工程师是百万量级。

02Why fine-tune for TRIZ?为什么值得为 TRIZ 做微调

Prior LLM+TRIZ work was all prompt engineering. Fine-tuning buys three things prompts can't: terminology discipline (correct principle numbering and analysis steps, baked into weights rather than begged for in prompts), behavioral style (direct, executable answers — our fine-tunes compress base responses to ~1/10 length at parity coverage), and internalized capability (the difference between consulting a book and knowing the method).

此前的 LLM+TRIZ 工作全部是提示词工程。微调能买到三样提示词买不到的东西：术语纪律（原理编号引用、分析步骤完整性，固化进权重而不是在 prompt 里恳求）、行为风格（直接可执行的回答——我们的微调把基座回答压缩到约 1/10 长度且覆盖持平）、能力内嵌（"查书答题"与"学会方法"的区别）。

It turns methodology application from a per-day-billed service into infrastructure with zero marginal cost.

微调模型把方法论的应用能力，从按人天计费的服务变成边际成本为零的基础设施。

For enterprises, the innovation bottleneck is rarely ideas — it is problem formulation. A resident model answers "which principles apply to my contradiction?" at 2am before the design review; it scales the onboarding tier (our strongest task types are exactly principle recommendation and concept explanation); and it drafts contradiction-analysis reports so expert time shifts from production to judgment. An assistant, not a replacement — but it raises the floor for everyone.

对企业而言，创新的瓶颈往往不是点子，而是问题表述。常驻模型能在设计评审前夜凌晨两点回答"我的矛盾该用哪几条原理"；它把入门普及层规模化（我们最强的子集恰是原理推荐与概念讲解）；它起草矛盾分析与 ARIZ 报告，让专家时间从"生产"转向"判断"。这是助手而非替代——但它抬高了所有人的下限。

04The evaluation caught itself lying评测系统抓到了自己说谎

Three measurement failures found and repaired during five versions of iteration — each capable of reversing a conclusion:

五版本迭代中发现并修复的三类测量学故障——每一类都足以反转结论：

1

A baseline that was lying

会说谎的基线

The harness stripped the empty think block the thinking-native base needs; base emitted unterminated reasoning drafts on 91/100 gold items. Both tracks were grading drafts.

harness 剥离了 thinking-native 基座所需的空 think 块；基座在 91/100 题上输出未闭合思考草稿——两条评分轨评的都是草稿。

+1.00 → −0.30

2

Judge position bias at 2× the literature amplitude

评委位置偏差达文献振幅两倍

The pairwise judge picked the second-presented candidate 81% of the time (0.87 inconsistency; literature ~25pp). Single-order pairwise is noise; dual-order merging is now enforced in code. Bonus: at T=0 the judge was fully deterministic (0.000 flip rate).

pairwise 评委 81% 选择第二位候选（不一致率 0.87，文献约 25pp）。单序结论全是噪声，双序合并已以代码强制。附带发现：T=0 下评委完全确定（翻转率 0.000）。

0.87 [0.79, 0.92]

3

Two tracks voting opposite ways

两条轨投出相反的票

Keyword and judge tracks share ~10% variance. v3 once scored significantly better on keywords (+0.056) and significantly worse on the judge (−0.068) — it had learned to stuff keywords. Rule: release gates must be dual-track.

关键词轨与 judge 轨仅共享约 10% 方差。v3 曾关键词轨显著更好（+0.056）而 judge 轨显著更差（−0.068）——它学会了堆关键词。规则：发布门必须双轨。

dual-track or nothing

双轨，否则不下判

05Limitations, stated局限，如实声明

  • Single domain (Chinese TRIZ QA), single base, single PEFT config.
  • 单领域（中文 TRIZ 问答）、单基座、单 PEFT 配置。
  • Judge shares a family with the data generator (declared weak same-origin). A post-release external review (§03) confirmed direction under 2/3 external judges at ~1/4 the magnitude; same-family absolute scores are not portable across judge families.
  • 评委与数据生成器同族（已声明"同族弱异源"）。发布后异源终审（§03）在 2/3 外部评委下确认方向，但量级约为同族读数的 1/4；同族绝对分不可跨评委家族外推。
  • 100-item subsets are descriptive only; "tied with v2" means no significant difference detected, not proven equivalence.
  • 100 题口径子集仅为描述性；"与 v2 打平"意为未检出显著差异，而非已证等价。
  • Public benchmark withholds reference answers (copyright); keyword track fully reproducible, judge absolute scores not.
  • 公开基准不含参考答案（版权原因）；关键词轨可完整复现，judge 轨绝对分不可。
